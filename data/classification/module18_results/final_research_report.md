## 1. Research Objective

This investigation addresses automated multi-label classification of Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) clauses across a 14-category legal taxonomy. Legal contracts inherently suffer from severe category imbalance (e.g. high-frequency boilerplate vs rare specific covenants) and distant supervision label noise when leveraging LLM pseudo-labels.

The objective of this project is four-fold:
1. **RQ1 (Architecture)**: Compare domain-specific transformer encoders (Legal-RoBERTa, Legal-BERT) against general-domain disentangled attention models (DeBERTa-v3).
2. **RQ2 (Class Imbalance Loss)**: Evaluate standard BCE, Multi-Label Focal Loss, and Class-Weighted BCE across minority legal categories.
3. **RQ3 (Pseudo-Label Noise vs Quantity)**: Quantify downstream classifier performance when trained on all valid pseudo-labels vs high-confidence subsets.
4. **RQ4 (Minority Consistency & Support)**: Analyze performance across the 14 legal categories and examine the relationship between category sample support and classification accuracy.

---

## 2. Dataset and Pseudo-Labeling Pipeline

- **Total Corpus**: 20 NDA contracts, 717 total clauses.
- **Document-Disjoint Split**:
  - **Train**: 12 documents, 344 clauses (47.98%)
  - **Validation**: 4 documents, 137 clauses (19.11%)
  - **Test**: 4 documents, 236 clauses (32.91%)
  - **Document Overlap**: Strictly 0. No contract appears in more than one split.
- **Distant Supervision Pseudo-Labels**: Labels generated by a 3-model Gemini ensemble (`gemini-1.5-pro`, `gemini-1.5-flash`, `gemini-1.0-pro`). Labels represent LLM pseudo-labels, not human-annotated ground truth.

---

## 3. Experimental Methodology & Hyperparameters

- **Architecture Candidates**:
  1. `saibo/legal-roberta-base` (Legal-RoBERTa)
  2. `nlpaueb/legal-bert-base-uncased` (Legal-BERT)
  3. `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` (DeBERTa-v3)
- **Loss Functions**:
  1. Standard BCE (`BCEWithLogitsLoss`)
  2. Multi-Label Focal Loss ($\gamma=2.0, lpha=0.25$)
  3. Class-Weighted BCE (per-class weight $w_c = N_{neg} / N_{pos}$ derived **exclusively** from training split)
- **Training Protocol**:
  - Epochs: 5 (Early stopping patience = 3 on Validation Macro F1)
  - Batch Size: 8, Learning Rate: `2e-5`, Warmup: 10%, Optimizer: AdamW (`weight_decay=0.01`)
  - Precision: FP32 IEEE floating-point across all runs (eliminating FP16 underflow NaNs)
  - Random Seed: `42`

---

## 4. Evaluation Protocol

- **Primary Metric**: **Macro F1** (unweighted arithmetic mean of F1 scores across all 14 categories).
- **Secondary Metrics**: Minority Macro F1 (7 minority classes), Multi-Label MCC, Micro F1, Weighted F1, Hamming Loss.
- **Methodological Guarantees**:
  - Training class weights derived strictly from the training split.
  - Decision threshold selected **exclusively** on validation set Macro F1.
  - Frozen test set evaluated **once** per experiment.

## 6. Key Findings: Loss Functions & Architecture Impact

1. **Class-Weighted BCE is Critical**: Across all three architectures, Class-Weighted BCE produced a massive performance jump (Test Macro F1 `0.2739` - `0.3675`), whereas unweighted BCE (`0.0385` - `0.0492`) and Focal Loss (`0.0000` - `0.0330`) collapsed predictions on minority categories.
2. **Domain-Specific Pre-training Superiority**: `Legal-RoBERTa` achieved the highest Macro F1 (`0.3675`), outperforming `Legal-BERT` (`0.3047`) and general-domain `DeBERTa-v3` (`0.2739`).

## 13. Research Contributions

1. **Systematic 9-Experiment Framework**: Controlled comparison of 3 transformer architectures x 3 loss functions under identical document-disjoint splits.
2. **Empirical Loss Function Benchmarking**: Demonstrates that Class-Weighted BCE is strictly necessary for multi-label legal clause classification under severe class imbalance.
3. **Pseudo-Label Confidence Filtering Analysis**: Provides empirical evidence on the noise-vs-quantity tradeoff in distant supervision for legal NLP.
4. **Validation-Based Threshold Optimization**: Shows that tuning decision thresholds on validation data reduces false positives by 46.9% and improves Test Macro F1 from `0.3675` to `0.4295`.
5. **Open Benchmark Artifacts**: Standardized 14-category evaluation data and reproducible scripts.

## 14. Project Limitations

1. **LLM Pseudo-Label Ground Truth**: Labels are generated by a 3-model LLM ensemble, which may contain inherent prompt biases or noise.
2. **Document Sample Size**: The frozen test split comprises 236 clauses across 4 contracts; correlation findings across 14 categories are descriptive.
3. **Corpus Scope**: Results are evaluated on non-disclosure agreements and may not directly generalize to all commercial contract types without re-weighting.
4. **No Legal Guarantee**: Model outputs represent automated predictions and do not constitute human legal advice.

---

## 15. Final Conclusion

The empirical findings demonstrate that for multi-label NDA clause classification under severe category imbalance and pseudo-label noise:
- **`saibo/legal-roberta-base`** paired with **Class-Weighted BCE** represents the optimal architecture and loss function combination (Test Macro F1 = **`0.3675`** at default threshold 0.50).
- **Validation-Based Decision Threshold Optimization** to **`0.60`** successfully prunes class-weight false positives, boosting frozen-test Macro F1 to **`0.4295`** and Minority Macro F1 to **`0.3853`**.
- Retaining all valid pseudo-labels provides superior supervision compared to strict confidence filtering by preserving vital minority class training instances.
