# Module 14: Human Verification Strategy & Gold Standard Protocol

## Executive Summary

This report establishes the protocol for human expert verification of pseudo-labeled legal clauses.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RESEARCH ETHICS MANDATE**: **LLM pseudo-labels are NOT equivalent to human ground truth.** They represent automated pre-annotations generated by an LLM ensemble (`gemini-3.6-flash`, `gemini-3.5-flash`, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`). Final paper performance metrics reported in published literature MUST be evaluated against human-verified gold labels for the test split.

---

## 1. Stratified Sampling Methodology

To ensure representative coverage without requiring 100% manual annotation of the corpus, a stratified sampling protocol is defined across five dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Category Stratification
- Ensure representation across all 14 NDA categories.
- Over-sample minority categories (*Liability for Damages*, *Competition Rights*, *Intellectual Property*, *Governing Law and Jurisdiction*) to ensure $\ge 10$ verified clause instances per category.

### Dimension 2: Confidence Tier Stratification
- **HIGH_CONFIDENCE (3/3 Consensus)**: Sample 40% of verification set to measure precision of unanimous predictions.
- **MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE (2/3 Consensus)**: Sample 35% of verification set to evaluate majority voting accuracy.
- **LOW_CONFIDENCE / DISAGREEMENT**: Sample 25% of verification set to analyze failure modes and edge cases.

### Dimension 3: Document Diversity Stratification
- Sample clauses across all represented documents ($N_{\text{docs}} \ge 11$) to prevent single-document reviewer bias.

---

## 2. Recommended Sample Size & Human Annotation Protocol

- **Target Sample Size**: **100 clauses** for initial verification auditing; **100% of the Test Split** (~75-100 clauses) for published benchmark evaluation.
- **Reviewer Specifications**: 2 independent legal expert annotators (law students or legal professionals).
- **Inter-Annotator Agreement**: Measured using **Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$)** for binary category presence and **Fleiss' Kappa** across multi-label choices. Target agreement: $\kappa \ge 0.80$.
- **Adjudication Process**:
  - Unanimous human agreements ($A = B$) form the Ground Truth Gold Set.
  - Human reviewer disagreements ($A \neq B$) are resolved by a senior legal supervisor.
