# Module 6, 6B & 6C — Multi-Label Classification Infrastructure & Experiment Design Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RESEARCH & EVALUATION METHODOLOGY DISCLOSURES**:
> 1. **LLM PSEUDO-LABELS**: All target labels in this pipeline are **LLM pseudo-labels** generated by a 3-model ensemble (Gemini 3.6 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite). They are automated pre-annotations for machine learning experimentation and **MUST NOT be claimed as human ground-truth labels**.
> 2. **TEMPORARY DEBUGGING SPLIT**: The current document-disjoint split (203 clauses across 7 documents) is **TEMPORARY / FOR PIPELINE DEBUGGING ONLY**. Because 13 of the 14 categories are concentrated in just 2 documents (`0859334b` and `0b59dfc4`), strict document-disjoint evaluation on the current 7 documents leaves the Test set with insufficient category coverage. **No results on this temporary split are reported as final research findings.**
> 3. **OBJECTIVE EMPIRICAL REPORTING**: All future model comparison reports (`compare_experiments.py`) will present measured empirical metrics without subjective ranking terminology (e.g. 'best', 'winner', 'superior').
> 4. **PAUSE ON FULL TRAINING**: Full multi-epoch research training is intentionally paused until Gemini API quota resets, pseudo-labeling completes across all 717 V3 clauses (20+ documents), and a balanced 14-category document-disjoint benchmark split is constructed.
