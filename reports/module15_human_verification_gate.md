# Module 15: Human Verification Gate & Sampling Protocol

## Executive Summary

This report specifies the human verification gate criteria, sampling strategy, and agreement calculation protocols required before publishing research findings.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ESSENTIAL DISTINCTION**: **LLM pseudo-labels are NOT equivalent to human ground truth.** LLM pseudo-labels are automated pre-annotations generated by a 3-model ensemble. Human verification converts pseudo-labels into a gold-standard benchmark.

---

## 1. Human Verification Gate Status

- **Status**: **CONDITIONALLY READY**
- **Artifact Prepared**: `data/annotations/human_verification_sample.csv` (30 clauses stratified across categories and confidence tiers).
- **Execution Target**: Human expert verification MUST be conducted on the Test split (~75-100 clauses) prior to paper submission.

---

## 2. Stratified Sampling Dimensions

1. **Minority Category Sampling**: Over-sample candidate clauses for *Liability for Damages*, *Competition Rights*, *Intellectual Property*, and *Governing Law* to achieve $\ge 10$ human-verified instances per minority category.
2. **Disagreement & Low Confidence Sampling**: Include 100% of model disagreement cases (`DISAGREEMENT`) and low-confidence predictions to measure edge-case accuracy.
3. **Confidence Tier Representation**:
   - 40% HIGH_CONFIDENCE
   - 35% MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE
   - 25% LOW_CONFIDENCE / DISAGREEMENT
4. **Document Representation**: Sample clauses across all represented documents ($N_{\text{docs}} \ge 11$).

---

## 3. Inter-Annotator Agreement & Adjudication

- **Metric**: Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$) for binary category presence.
- **Passing Threshold**: $\kappa \ge 0.80$ across 2 independent human legal annotators.
- **Adjudication Rule**: Disagreements between human annotators are resolved by a senior legal expert whose decision becomes final ground truth.
